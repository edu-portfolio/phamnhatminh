About Me

Curiosity-Driven.
Research-Focused.

My family runs a fertilizer company in Vietnam, so my first contact with chemistry was not a textbook but a technical department: raw materials, pH checks, and the question of what actually happens to a crop after a farmer uses a product.

That question has shaped what I do outside class. I have worked on enzyme and analytical chemistry projects in a teaching laboratory, presented two team research projects in Seoul, spent time on a specialty coffee farm, and started small projects that explain soil science to students and farmers.

I try to report my results with their limits. Several of my lab reports end with what the experiment could not prove and what the next study would need, because I think that is part of doing science honestly.
